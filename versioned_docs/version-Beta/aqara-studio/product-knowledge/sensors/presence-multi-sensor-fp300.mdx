# Presence Multi-Sensor FP300

<img src="https://itad-aqara-docs-1300889962.aqara.com/dev/img/aqara-studio/zh/presence-multi-sensor-fp300-fp300-cover.png" alt="Presence Multi-Sensor FP300" style={{width: '50%'}} />

## Product Introduction

The Presence Multi-Sensor FP300 is a battery-powered sensor that combines millimeter-wave radar, infrared detection, and Aqara AI algorithms to detect human presence, even when a person is still. With spatial learning and adaptive sensitivity, it adjusts to its surroundings while also monitoring temperature, humidity, and ambient light. It supports both Thread and Zigbee.

Add the FP300 via the Aqara Studio web platform or the Aqara Life App. For Matter, use a compatible Matter controller or hub. For Zigbee, use a compatible Zigbee hub.

Switching protocols clears all settings and data on the device. Record any settings you want to keep before switching.

## Device Installation

The device supports wall mounting, tabletop placement, and ceiling mounting. Choose a location for the area you want to monitor, then adjust the mounting angle and the device tilt. You can secure the device with adhesive, a magnetic mount, or screws. Use screws for installations above 2 m.

### Installation Recommendations

- When mounting the device on a wall, in a corner, or on a ceiling, point the front toward the area you want to monitor and adjust the angle as needed.
- For wider coverage, install the device about 2 m above the floor and tilt it slightly downward toward the main activity area.
- For side mounting on a wall or in a corner, a height of 1.4–1.8 m is recommended to cover both standing and seated occupants. If you install it at another height, adjust the bracket tilt to cover the activity area.
- For corner mounting, point the front along the centerline of the corner to reduce blind spots on either side at longer distances. The base has an offset swivel joint. Rotate the base to increase the available tilt.
- Avoid pointing the device directly at large reflective surfaces such as metal or glass. Keep it away from air conditioner vents, air purifiers, and fans.
- Use adhesive or magnetic mounting below 2 m. Above 2 m, secure the device with screws.

## Setting the Radar Detection Range

The device detects up to 6 m away at a detection angle of 120° under ideal laboratory conditions. Actual coverage depends on the room, mounting height, and angle. The maximum distance may be shorter toward the edges of the detection area, where the radar signal is weaker.

Adjust the detection range in 0.25 m increments on the device settings page. You can exclude specific distance intervals within the range.

The person icon in the settings shows a reference value for **Current Position**. This is the straight-line distance from the device to the point on the person giving the strongest radar return. Use it as a guide when setting the detection boundary. Beyond 6 m, the readout shows **Unknown**.

To set the range, slowly approach the device and stop at the boundary of the area you want to monitor. Wait for the distance reading to stabilize. Set the range using that reading, then check the presence status and adjust the range as needed. Radar range settings require an active radar mode.

## Absence Confirmation Period

The absence confirmation period is how long the device must continuously detect no presence before reporting absence. A shorter period reports absence sooner, but environmental interference is more likely to affect detection. In most cases, start with the default setting.

If you set an absence confirmation period and an automation duration, they run one after the other. For example, set the period to 5 seconds and configure the lights to turn off after 10 seconds with no presence detected. The device first confirms and reports absence, then the automation waits another 10 seconds before turning off the lights.

## AI Spatial Learning

AI Spatial Learning helps the device adapt to interference from reflective surfaces such as large panes of glass, metal, and mirrors. After installation, run it manually once and keep the detection area unoccupied for about 30 seconds.

The device continues to learn as you use it. If interference causes the device to keep reporting presence in an empty room, run AI Spatial Learning again with the room unoccupied.

## Recommended Automations

- Turn on the lights when presence is detected.  
- Turn off the lights or air conditioner when no presence is detected for a set duration.  
- Turn on the lights when presence is detected and the light level is below a set threshold.  
- When presence is detected and the temperature is above a set threshold, turn on the air conditioner in cooling mode and set it to 24 °C.

## Tips

- Point the device toward the front of the body to help it detect subtle movements such as breathing.
- The device has a detection range of up to 6 m. Set the range for the area you want to monitor.
- To keep the lights from switching on and off too often, adjust the Absence Confirmation Period or use **No presence detected for a certain duration** as your automation trigger.
- If the device reports presence in an empty room, run AI Spatial Learning with the room unoccupied.
- Keep the device away from air conditioner vents, air purifiers, and fans.
- The device needs time to learn a new environment. If you notice occasional false reports after installation, leave the room unoccupied while it learns. If detection is still unreliable, adjust the mounting angle or move the device.

## Reset the Device

A device reset clears network settings and returns the device to pairing mode while keeping local data. With the device powered on, press and hold the reset button for about 5 seconds. Release it when the indicator flashes rapidly.

## Factory Reset

A factory reset clears network settings and deletes all local and cloud data. With the device powered on, quickly press the reset button 10 times to restore factory settings.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Detection and False Triggers

#### Why does my FP300 still report presence after I leave?

The device switches to absence after it continuously detects no presence for the entire confirmation period. If the room is quiet and the change is simply slow, try setting the period to 5 seconds, or 1 second if available. If the device still reports presence, check for swaying curtains, moving light fixtures, or drafts and follow the first set of steps in Troubleshooting.

#### How can I tell a false infrared motion event from a false presence report?

**Infrared motion detected** is a PIR event that can be triggered by temperature changes, drafts, or direct sunlight. **Presence detected** is the occupancy status. In Dual Presence Mode, if the device continues to show presence in an empty room, check the radar detection area for interference. For occupancy-based lighting, use the presence or absence status as the trigger. Occasional false PIR triggers usually have little effect on battery life.

#### How can I reduce false absence reports when someone is sitting still or sleeping?

Aim the device toward the person's chest and keep the person within the detection area with a clear line of sight. Try High sensitivity and extend the absence confirmation period as needed. Start with Medium sensitivity in regularly used rooms such as a living room or study, and try Low sensitivity in hallways or bathrooms where people tend to stay briefly.

#### Does the FP300 support pet filtering?

The FP300 has no dedicated pet filter or weight-based pet filtering. Adjust the mounting angle and detection range to avoid areas on the floor where pets often move. Medium or large pets may still trigger detection.

#### What can the FP300 detect?

The FP300 detects human presence, including people who are still. It also monitors temperature, humidity, and ambient light.

### Installation and Placement

#### Where should I install the FP300, and how high should it be?

Place it on a tabletop or mount it on a wall or ceiling. For side mounting on a wall or in a corner, install it 1.4–1.8 m above the floor facing the main activity area. For wider coverage, mount it about 2 m high and tilt it slightly downward. Use screws above 2 m.

#### Why does the actual detection range differ from the stated range?

The stated range of up to 6 m at 120° was measured under ideal laboratory conditions. Mounting height and angle, posture, obstructions, and reflections can affect coverage. Test the paths people use and check that key areas such as doorways fall within the detection area.

#### How can I mount the device so it detects someone entering sooner?

In Dual Presence Mode, aim the device so people entering a doorway or passing through a hall cross the PIR detection area from the side. Test it on the paths people use and adjust the angle as needed.

#### How can I reduce interference from glass, metal, or airflow?

Avoid large glass, metal, or mirrored surfaces and keep the device away from air conditioner vents, fans, and air purifiers. Adjust the angle so the radar faces away from reflective surfaces. After moving the device, run AI Spatial Learning with the room unoccupied.

### Pairing and Maintenance

#### Which protocols does the FP300 support, and how do I add it?

The FP300 supports Thread and Zigbee. With the device in pairing mode, use **Add Device** in the Aqara Life App. In Aqara Studio, go to **Devices** and select **Matter** or **Zigbee**, then follow the on-screen instructions. Use a hub or controller compatible with the selected protocol.

#### How do I update the firmware?

After adding the device in the Aqara Life App, check for updates on the device settings page. See **Device Pairing** for firmware update and protocol switching instructions.

#### What are the operating temperature and humidity ranges?

The operating temperature is -10 to 50 °C. The operating humidity is 0–95% RH, non-condensing.
